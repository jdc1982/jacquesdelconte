Jacques del Conte

Executive Producer & Post Production Consultant

Jacques del Conte is an executive producer and post production consultant working at the intersection of fashion, beauty, and film. He partners with directors, agencies, and houses to shepherd projects through post — from edit and color through finishing and delivery — keeping the creative intent intact at every stage.

His work spans campaigns for some of the most recognizable names in luxury and beauty, including Louis Vuitton, Dior, Cartier, Gucci, Armani Beauty, Zara, Mugler, Alaïa, Zegna, Lancôme, and Dolce & Gabbana, in collaboration with directors such as Fabien Baron, Brigitte Lacombe, Damien Chazelle, and Roman Coppola.
