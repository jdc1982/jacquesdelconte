About

Jacques del Conte

Executive Producer, Post Production

I produce post for luxury fashion and beauty films, working directly with brand studios and with the agencies that make their campaigns. You get one bid, one invoice and one person responsible from the first cut through colour, beauty retouch and final delivery.

Every project is built around the artists that film actually needs, drawn from a bench of editors, colourists and finishing artists I have hired and worked with for years, and bid from real production cost data.

I worked many years as a commercial editor in New York, so I know the cutting room from the inside. I also built two in-house post companies from nothing: Baron & Baron's post production facility and Unreasonable Studios at Anomaly. In both I set up the pipeline, hired the team and ran post across the agency's client roster.

Before post, I worked in the art department at Vanity Fair and studied photography at Bard College under Stephen Shore and Larry Fink, whose instinct for the image shaped how I think about every frame.
