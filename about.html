About

Jacques del Conte

Executive Producer & Post Production Consultant

I run post production for brands, agencies and creative directors, consolidating a complex workflow into a single point of accountability and building each team from the artists the film actually needs.

I have built two in-house post production companies from nothing: 50 Frames at Baron & Baron and Unreasonable Studios at Anomaly. In both I set up the pipeline, hired the team and ran post across the agency's full client roster. I now build that same operation around a single project at a time.

My career began in the art department at Vanity Fair and as a photographer. I came up under Stephen Shore and Larry Fink, whose instinct for the image shaped how I think about every frame. That range, from still photography to global 4K pipelines across colour, sound, VFX and finishing, is what lets me hold a project from the first conversation through to the last delivery.
