About

Jacques del Conte

Executive Producer & Post Production Consultant

I'm an Executive Producer specializing in Post Production, with an eye toward bridging creative ambitions with entrepreneurial goals. I founded two post production companies, 50 Frames at Baron & Baron and Unreasonable Studios at Anomaly.

I've worked across magazine, film, digital content, and advertising, starting my career in the art department of Vanity Fair Magazine and working as a photographer. I came up under mentors like Stephen Shore and Larry Fink, whose eye for image and instinct shaped how I think about every frame.

That range, from still photography to global 4K pipelines spanning color, sound, VFX, and finishing, is what lets me see a project from every angle and bring even the most complex creative ambitions to life.
